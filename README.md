# Tasnia Akther - Portfolio

Welcome to my personal portfolio! This portfolio showcases my skills, projects, and journey as a passionate software engineering student.

## Overview

This portfolio serves as an online representation of my work, including details about my skills, academic projects, and research. The project also demonstrates my proficiency in web development with HTML, CSS, and some JavaScript elements.

## Features

- **Home**: Introduction and greeting with an animated name.
- **About Me**: A brief overview of my academic background and key skills.
- **Resume**: A detailed section about my skills, both soft and technical, along with my ongoing projects and research paper.
- **Portfolio**: A showcase of my major projects and contributions.
- **Contact**: Information on how to reach me via email or social media platforms.

## Technologies Used

- **HTML5**
- **CSS3**
- **Bootstrap**
- **Font Awesome**
